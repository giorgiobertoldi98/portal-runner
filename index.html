Portal Runner

Every portal opens onto a world no one has mapped. Each hides dark gems, and something lives there that won't let you take them easily.

  • Hunt the dark gems hidden in every world.
  • Carry them to the portal. Each one slows you down.
  • Fill the portal and step through to the next world.
WASD or arrows to move, Shift to run, drag to look, Q to drop a gem, Enter or a click to take things, wheel and Tab to pick and read what you find, Esc to pause
Left thumb moves (push to the edge to run), right thumb looks, the tool buttons above the right thumb drop a gem, take things and open what you find
